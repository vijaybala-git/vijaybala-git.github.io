WhyWatt? Home Electrification Explorer

WhyWatt? Home Electrification Explorer (HEX) is an open-development, data-driven modeling platform built for California homeowners and clean energy advocates. Developed in Python with the Mesa agent simulation library and Solara, it models a household's complete energy balance—including heat pump HVAC and water heaters, induction cooktops, EV chargers, rooftop solar, and home battery storage.

Rather than promoting vendor equipment, WhyWatt projects two 20-year financial outcomes side by side: Your Journey of phased clean upgrades versus a Do Nothing baseline where gas appliances are replaced like-for-like as utility rates rise. By combining NREL hourly load shapes, local climate zones, and real utility time-of-use tariffs, it reveals exactly when and how clean energy pays for itself.
