Open Development & Community Input

Join & Contribute to the Development

WhyWatt? HEX is built through open development with community input. Methods, data, and testing procedures are openly shared. Join our development channel on Discord to review specifications, or volunteer to get access to code and support development.

Join the #development Channel on Discord

Join our dedicated #development channel on Discord to review architecture specifications, inspect implementation specs, discuss appliance physics, and participate in technical design discussions with the core team.

Volunteer to Get Access to Code & Support It

We welcome developers, energy modelers, and testers. Volunteer to get access to the Python codebase (Mesa agent simulation engine & Solara frontend) to help implement features, optimize performance, or add utility rate parsers.

Review Specifications & Methodology

Methods, data pipelines, and algorithms are openly documented. Audit the 5 offline pipelines (CEC weather, NREL load curves, URDB tariffs, PVWatts solar, and rate escalation models) and 9 appliance thermodynamics.

Testing Procedures & Real-Home Validation

Testing procedures and calibration benchmarks are openly shared. Help validate simulation outputs against real utility bills, contractor heat pump quotes, panel capacity calculations, and battery dispatch logs.
